## What was built

- **`Shared/Config/MachineVoice.luau`** (new, mapped in `default.project.json`): 58 original lines in six categories, `MachineVoice.pick(category, rng, ctx)` and `MachineVoice.roleCard(trialId, roleName, playerName)`. Pure Lua 5.1 (loads in the tests without Roblox globals). `pick` accepts a Roblox `Random` (`NextInteger`) or a plain `rng(n)` function; lines may carry `<NAME>` (from `ctx.name`, uppercased, cut to 14 like the standings rows) and `<DIR>` (`ctx.dir = "up"|"down"`); a line whose placeholder has no value is not a candidate, so nothing ever prints a raw `<NAME>`.

  | category | lines | fired when |
  |---|---:|---|
  | outcome | 12 | default for every standings / score / final packet |
  | swing | 10 | a rank changed hands (standings: vs previous round; score: session place vs previous trial) |
  | streak | 9 | the same subject alone on top twice+ in a row (standings), or the trial winner also won the previous trial (score) |
  | firstdeath | 8 | the session's first body, spoken once (standings, or score if it fell in the trial's last round) |
  | sweep | 9 | everyone but one went down this round; one subject alone on top in every round of the trial (score); one subject won every trial (final) |
  | idle | 10 | pool only — not wired (see "Not done") |

  Priority when several apply: firstdeath > sweep > streak > swing > outcome.
