## Live-check list for the lead

1. **Typewriter speed is frame-rate independent.** In Studio set the frame cap to 30 fps (File → Studio Settings → Rendering → "Frame Rate Cap" or `--fps`-style setting) and play one round card (`Round n/m` verbs) and one Status screen; then at 60 fps. The verb line (≈ 24 graphemes at 24 cps) must finish in ≈ 1.0 s at both caps; a Status row of 30 characters in ≈ 1.0 s at both. Under the old loop 30 fps typed ~40 % slower. Screens to watch: Selection boot text ("Simulation bootst:"), Info "NEXT SIMULATION: …" (now 24 cps, was 26), Status rows (30 cps), RoundCard verbs (24 cps), Announce / death line (24 cps), the score voice line (30 cps).
2. **Blip.** With UI sound on, a soft `Hover` tick every 3 graphemes at varying pitch; with `UI SOUND: OFF` no ticks. Check no `Sound` instances accumulate under `SoundService` after a few screens (clones destroy on `Ended`).
3. **Cancel semantics.** Let the Machine cut from Status to RoundCard mid-line: the older line must freeze, not keep typing over the new screen.
4. **Roulette.** Tile chrome steps DIM↔PHOSPHOR at ≈ 3 changes/s during the 1.6 s chaos phase, icons stay visible; 3 slow lock blinks; crosshair snap 0.32 s; whole sequence ends before the `NEXT SIMULATION` card (≈ 5.5 s of the 6.4 s hold). With `FLICKER: REDUCED` the chaos phase is a 0.6 s hold and one blink.
5. **3-2-1 in all four places** (birdhunt, minefield, canteen, a kit trial such as sorting): count punches 160 → 120 px in 0.3 s, GO 150 → 118 px in 0.35 s and hides 0.7 s later; one count per 1.0 s. The arena fades in under "3" (1.4 s).
6. **Canteen HUD glow** on a phone emulation (Platform=Mobile): warn/off glow 0.25 s; on desktop 0.175 s; danger snaps in 0.12 s on both.
7. **Death.** Card holds 2.5 s on the body, blacks out over 0.5 s, spectate camera lands at 3.2 s.
8. **Odometer.** Score digits roll at a visible 12 fps step, land in order (0.45 s apart) as before.
9. **Overlay.** Refresh band every 10 s (8 idle + 2 sweep), blip every 8–20 s — unchanged timing, now from FeelConfig.
