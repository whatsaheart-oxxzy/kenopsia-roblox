- **`StarterPlayerScripts/GradeDirector.client.luau`** (new): successor to the
  archived SimulationGrade. Key mechanics:
  - Grade keyed on **`KenopsiaTrialId`** (verified live: MachineFlow:369
    `plr:SetAttribute("KenopsiaTrialId", trialId or "")`) so the look holds
    across standings cards and death/respawn; the old module's re-apply on every
    round card was a visible pop. `KenopsiaActiveTrial` (round flag) gates
    quantisation and serves as a fallback trigger.
  - Restore proved in TWO halves on read-back values (exact `==`, no recomputed
    Color3s): (1) pre-restore drift check against the post-apply read-back —
    catches third-party Lighting writes; (2) post-restore write-back compare —
    catches clamped/failed writes. Either sets Lighting attribute
    `GradeRestoreMismatch = trialId`. Known-legitimate exemption: LightGuard's
    one-shot ambient bump (stamped `KenopsiaLowLight`) may drift Ambient/
    OutdoorAmbient mid-grade on a late-joining phone; fog drift is never exempt.
  - Camera quantisation splits by camera type: monolith's RenderStepped writer
    still consumes `KenopsiaCamQuant` for Scriptable cameras (hook kept 27.08 as
    the re-arm point); GradeDirector adds the archive's BindToRenderStep
    (priority Last) for the Custom camera only — the two can never fight.
    Suspended in sniper scope (`KenopsiaMachine.Scope.Visible`) and under
    Reduce Motion (OS flag OR `KenopsiaReduceFlicker`).
  - No Atmosphere, no sky swap, no lamp rasters in Phase 2; `KenopsiaClient`
    untouched (register ceiling).
- **`tests/grade.lua`** (new): 54 checks, 0 failures under real Lua 5.1.5
  (`C:\Lua\bin\lua.exe`), run from repo root; `tests/feel.lua` control run
