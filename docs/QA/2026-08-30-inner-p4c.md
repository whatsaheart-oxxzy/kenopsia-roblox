  carries both facts, so the change stays inside `applyQuests` and its one caller.

## The defect the review caught (and fixed)

The staged wallet roll-up was `while fragments >= perKey do ... end`. Because
`recordSession` never yields, a corrupted numeric counter would **not throw — it
would spin**, freezing the whole room's verdict thread (`1e9` ≈ 2×10⁸ iterations;
`math.huge` or `perKey < 1` never terminates). Strictly worse than the throw the
surrounding guards were written to prevent. **Proven, not theorised**: the
reviewer drove the real `recordSession` under Lua 5.1 with
`crates.fragments = 1e9` — as staged it timed out at 30 s (exit 124); fixed it
completes in <1 ms with identical arithmetic. Fix: one O(1) division plus finite
guards that scrub NaN/inf/negative (values that cannot round-trip a DataStore and
would otherwise silently kill every future write for that player).
